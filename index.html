Instructions & Notes

• Dissolve yeast in room temperature water.

• Drop flour into the yeast/water mixture.

• Mix until a loose dough forms using one of the mixer hooks. (Dough should be just a little bit sticky/shaggy).

• Cover bowl with saran wrap and let sit for 30–60 minutes (Autolyse).

• Add salt to the autolysed dough.

• Attach hook to mixer. Mix at speed 2 for 4 minutes.

• Add EV Olive Oil.

• Mix at speed 2 for another 4 minutes.

Pro Note: Adding salt before oil creates a softer crust. Adding oil before salt creates a crunchier crust!

• Divide dough evenly (~390g each approx.).

• Shape each dough into a boule by gently pushing up the center and pinching off the bottom. Bottom must be sealed.

• Put in Tupperware with a lightly oiled bottom and cover.

• Keep out for 30 mins and then place in the fridge for up to 3 days (48hr cold rise).

• Crucial: Take dough out of the fridge a MINIMUM of 90 minutes before using (usually 2 hours before using). Never stretch cold dough!

Pizza Sauce (Cooked) 6 Pizzas

Ingredients & Directions:

  • San Marzano canned tomatoes (plum peeled w/ basil leaf) — Add fresh basil leaves, blend with hand mixer.
  • Cook garlic in vegetable oil until browned.
  • Add 796 ml can of blended tomato sauce.
  • Add 100 ml of water.
  • Add 1/2 teaspoon (2.5 ml) of salt.
  • Bring to boil at medium/high heat (5 mins).
  • Lower heat and simmer for 15 minutes.

Pizza Sauce (Fresh) 6 Pizzas

Ingredients:

  • San Marzano canned tomatoes (plum peeled)
  • Sea Salt (2.5 ml for 796 ml can)
  • Fresh Basil Leaf(s)
  • Olive oil (if you want a bit extra flavor)

Directions:

• Hand mixer. Blend lightly (not fine / keep very small pieces for texture).

Golden Notes & Pro Tips

  • Yeast conversion: Instant yeast grams to ml — multiply by 1.56.
  • Whole wheat rule: Use 100g of whole wheat bread flour per total flour weight for each dough.
  • Peel prep: Use semola on pizza peel instead of flour so it doesn't stick.
  • Baking: Use convection oven at 550°F (or as high as oven goes) on the lowest level in the oven.
  • Toppings: Use part-skim mozzarella. Pre-cook veggies or fatty meats (bacon, sausage, etc.).
  • Yeast safety: Adding yeast to water that's too warm or having too much yeast might cause over-fermentation.
  • Crust texture: Adding salt before oil creates a softer crust. Adding oil before salt creates a crunchier crust.
