Instructions & Notes

• Dissolve yeast in room temperature water.

• Drop flour into the yeast/water mixture.

• Mix until a loose dough forms using mixer hooks (dough should be sticky/shaggy).

• Cover bowl with saran wrap and let sit for 30–60 minutes (Autolyse).

• Add salt to the autolysed dough.

• Attach hook to mixer. Mix at speed 2 for 4 minutes.

• Add EV Olive Oil.

• Mix at speed 2 for another 4 minutes.

Pro Note: Adding salt before oil creates a softer crust. Adding oil before salt creates a crunchier crust!

• Divide dough evenly (~390g each approx.).

• Shape each dough into a boule by gently pushing up center and pinching off bottom. Bottom must be sealed.

• Put in Tupperware with lightly oiled bottom and cover.

• Keep out for 30 mins and place in fridge for up to 3 days (48hr cold rise).

• Crucial: Take dough out of fridge MINIMUM 90 minutes before using (usually 2 hours before using). Never stretch cold dough.

Pizza Sauce (Cooked) 6 Pizzas

  • San Marzano canned tomatoes (plum peeled w/ basil leaf) + fresh basil leaves. Hand mixer blend.
  • Cook garlic in vegetable oil until browned.
  • Add 796 ml can of sauce, 100 ml water, 1/2 tsp (2.5 ml) salt.
  • Bring to boil at medium/high heat (5 mins), lower heat and simmer for 15 mins.

Pizza Sauce (Fresh) 6 Pizzas

  • San Marzano canned tomatoes (plum peeled), Sea Salt (2.5ml for 796ml can), Basil Leaf(s), Olive oil for extra flavor.
  • Hand mixer: blend lightly (not fine / keep very small pieces).

Golden Notes & Pro Tips

  • Yeast conversion: Instant yeast grams to ml — multiply by 1.56.
  • Whole wheat rule: Use 100g of whole wheat bread flour per total wheat for each dough.
  • Peel prep: Use semola on pizza peel instead of flour so it doesn't stick.
  • Baking: Use convection oven at 550°F (or as high as oven goes) on the lowest level in the oven.
  • Toppings: Part-skim mozzarella. Pre-cook veggies or fatty meats (bacon, sausage, etc.).
